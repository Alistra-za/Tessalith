## 3.1.0

- BC1, BC3, BC4 and BC5 encode more than four times as fast, with the same files byte for byte: 300 textures as BC1 take about 14 seconds where 3.0.0 took over a minute.
- BC7 and BC6H encode about 1.5 times as fast on CPUs with AVX-512. Their bytes differ a little from an AVX2 CPU's, at the same quality. On CPUs without AVX2 they are slower than before: I dropped the AVX and SSE4 code to keep the .exe small, which only affects CPUs more than 10 years old that lack AVX2.
- BC6H (HDR sky and PBR emissive) gets four encoder fixes: closer to the source at the default effort (about +0.07 dB), and 2 to 5 % slower. The two fastest efforts lose a hair (0.003 dB), and I kept the fixes anyway because every slower effort gains. Existing BC6H files are kept; to encode them again, run `sdc convert <project> --on-existing if-changed --ev --property bc6h_uf16`.
- New **Max** effort for BC3, BC4 and BC5. What 3.0.0 called `max` is now **Slowest**: a project or preset that says `max` gets the new, slower search, and its BC3, BC4 and BC5 files are encoded again on the next run. Set it to `slowest` first to keep them.
- Also new: Alpha ×4, ×32 and ×64 BC7 weighting, and a `fast` BC1 search that takes about a third of the time. It still beats NVTT `-highest` on both decoder models, and it is faster than both texconv and NVTT's CUDA GPU path on my RTX 4090 with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D (I make no claim for other setups).
- `mips.normal_base = "keep"` now keeps non-unit-length normals across the whole mip chain: each smaller mip keeps the average authored length, as far as XYZ storage can hold it. The variance bake reads only the spread of directions, so the roughness it bakes is correct for them too, and a flat patch of short normals no longer gets rougher at mip 1. BC5 can't store a length, so Keep on a BC5 normal now fails with `CFG-INVALID`: choose BC7, or `renormalize`. Existing outputs re-encode only with `--ev`; the commands are in the changelog.
- In the GUI, normals follow their set's shader too: in a PBR or PBR Landscape set an untouched `_n` drops its alpha by default. If shared specular would be lost, Problems offers **Keep alpha**.
- GUI: Review opens before an export (tick **Export without review next time** to skip it), and Problems can reach zero (notes split out, **Mark as intended**, more one-click fixes). The Advanced drawer is gone: Size, Levels and DDS header are in the texture panel, and a new **Settings** tab holds Mode, Preset, the export folder and app settings. The 3D preview renders in under half the time.
- After an update, outputs whose encode version changed are kept and counted, not encoded again, until you ask with `--ev` or with **Re-encode every output** in the GUI's **Tools** menu.
- I turned validation after encoding off by default: the files are byte for byte the same either way, and exports finish sooner. Turn it on with `--validate` or **Validate exported DDS** in **Settings**. A script that relies on exit code 4 needs `--validate`, and one that matches message text needs updating: messages say "type" where they said "sub-type". Complex material `_m` and complex grass textures are still always validated, as a failure there would turn features off in game unexpectedly.
- Lots of smaller fixes in menus, lists, sharing, the `_f` and `_fuzz` suffix confusion, NIF edits and screen-reader names.
